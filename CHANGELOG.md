## - September 23, 2026 
### Huge Update!
- Now just under 500 offline cities total
- Type-to-filter (Y), page jump (L/R), jump-to-next-letter (ZL/ZR) for navigating the list
- Enter custom coordinates by hand (lat/lon + manual UTC offset) — works fully offline
- Save any location (city, custom coordinates, or a searched address) under a name for instant reuse later
- Now remembers your last-used location and launches straight into it
- 6 new calculation methods available: ISNA, Muslim World League, Umm Al-Qura, Karachi, Egyptian, Tehran, Diyanet
- Hanafi / Standard (Shafi) Asr toggle
- High-latitude adjustment: None (clamps to horizon), Middle of the Night, One-Seventh, Angle-Based (only kicks in where the plain calculation is actually impossible for the day/latitude)
- Now has Qibla direction from the specific location
- New Next-prayer countdown
- Monthly prayer timetable, scrollable, with L/R to flip months
- City list slightly simplified, you can revert this in the settings
